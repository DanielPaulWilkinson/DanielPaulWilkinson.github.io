Risk analysis

Identifies and prioritises software engineering risks across technical design, security, reliability, operations, dependencies, vendors, cost, scalability, migration, and implementation.

Consider

Technical

Compatibility, complexity, performance, maturity, integration.

Security

Attack surface, privilege, dependencies, data exposure, patching.

Reliability

Single points of failure, recovery, data loss, cascading failure.

Operational

Maintenance, support, deployment, observability, skills.

Dependency/vendor

Lock-in, licensing, project health, pricing, discontinuation.

Scalability

Capacity limits and architectural constraints.

Migration

Portability, rollback, data conversion, downtime, exit cost.

Financial

Build, run, licence, migration, hidden labour cost.

Rating

Likelihood: Low/Medium/High Impact: Low/Medium/High Overall: Low/Medium/High

Every material risk should have a mitigation or explicit acceptance.
