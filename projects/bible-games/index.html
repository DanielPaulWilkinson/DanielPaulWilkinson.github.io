Bible Game Night

A family Bible game night on one phone: 35 games, a 'Play now' line-up that ends with a blessing, and Bible verses quoted exactly from the BSB.

What it is

A family Bible game night that runs on one phone, with 35 games. 'Play now' deals tonight's three games, one to get moving, one to laugh and one calm one, and ends with a Blessing Round from Numbers 6:24–26.

  • A 'Who's playing?' choice: little ones with us, or grown-ups and friends.
  • Games grouped by mood, with age, time and 'From the Book of' badges.
  • Pass-the-phone turns and a winner celebration. Worship rounds are never scored.
  • Bible verses quoted exactly from the public-domain Berean Standard Bible (BSB).

Why I built it

I wanted games my boys could play with us in the evenings that are fun first and keep the real Bible text intact. It's designed around research on co-operative family play: one shared screen in the style of Jackbox or Heads Up, with people looking at each other rather than staring at screens.

How it's built

Vue 3 (Composition API), TypeScript and Vite, tested with Vitest (419 tests). Sounds are made with the Web Audio API. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, with no accounts and no tracking. Illustrations are by Sweet Publishing, used under CC BY-SA.

Status and limits

A personal project that's live and still growing. It's designed for one shared device rather than multiple phones.
